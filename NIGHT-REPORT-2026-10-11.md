## Needs your decision (no action taken)
1. **Internal RAM is the structural weak point.** Idle we have ~50 KB of the 320 KB internal SRAM free; a tap storm with four downloads queued exhausted it (SD writer task could not start, a TCP connect failed, then a crash). Tonight's mitigations (PSRAM threshold 2 KB, BLE memory release, AsyncTCP stack, debounce) help, but the real options are: drop Bluedroid for NimBLE (~30 KB), stop the Reverb TLS WebSocket from living in internal RAM (~60 KB at connect; switch to `esp_websocket_client`, or plain `ws://` through a reverse proxy on the LAN is not an option in production), smaller WiFi static buffers (throughput trade-off). Which direction do you want? This gates "production level" more than anything else.
2. **OTA policy.** Today the device installs whatever the backend offers (pin or channel/rollout), but only when idle (no story, no figure, no download, charging or ≥ 40 %). If the mobile app must ask the user first, the backend simply should not offer until the user accepted (set the pin from the app flow). Channels are strict (a beta hub never sees stable), mandatory updates force after one hour of waiting, a new image confirms itself after 60 s. Confirm or change.
3. **Version numbering.** `CONFIG_APP_PROJECT_VER` in sdkconfig is the single source (1.1.9 now); the backend derives version_code as major×10000+minor×100+patch. `tools/release.sh <version> [--upload]` bumps it, builds, and drops `releases/tilkietalkie-<version>.bin` with its sha256/size for the admin Firmware page.
4. **Private channel + server-side commands (backend branch merged on the bench, not pushed).** Events are dual-published on `device.{uuid}` and `private-device.{uuid}`; the `X-Client-Source` bypass is gone; `POST /api/hubs/{hub}/command` exists.
