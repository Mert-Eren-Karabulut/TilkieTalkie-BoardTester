- **Charger "bouncing" (red STAT LED blinking) — root cause, not a hardware fault:** every USB session started from the BQ25792 default input budget (3.3 A + ICO probing upwards). A plain 5 V adapter or a power bank collapses under that, VBUS drops out, the charger stops, the source recovers, repeat every few seconds; the firmware's "kick" logic and the bus-presence flicker made it worse.
